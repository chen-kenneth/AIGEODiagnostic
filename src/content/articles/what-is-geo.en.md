You've probably heard the term "GEO" many times. But what does it stand for? How does it relate to SEO? This article covers the fundamentals in one place.

## 1. What GEO Stands For

GEO stands for **Generative Engine Optimization**.

Breaking it down:

- **Generative Engine**: Tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude that "generate" answers. Unlike traditional search engines that return a list of links, they produce a composed answer directly.
- **Optimization**: Making your information easier for these AI engines to understand, cite, and recommend.

In one sentence: **SEO optimizes for search engines; GEO optimizes for AI answers.**

## 2. Why GEO Emerged

Because the way people find information has changed.

For the past decade, finding a realtor looked like this: open Google → search "Chinese-speaking realtor Toronto" → click through links one by one → compare yourself. SEO optimized that flow: getting your website ranked higher in search results.

Now, more and more people's flow looks like this: open ChatGPT → ask "which Chinese-speaking realtor is good for buying in Toronto" → get a few names with reasons directly. **There's no search results page in between, no chance for position #10 — the AI either mentions you or it doesn't.**

This shift creates new problems:

1. **No rankings to check**: AI answers have no fixed rankings. Ask the same question a few times and the list of mentioned names may differ.
2. **Different citation logic**: AI composes answers from sources it has "read and trusts." Your website ranking #1 doesn't mean the AI will cite you.
3. **The bilingual gap**: Ask in Chinese vs. English and the AI may return completely different lists. Covering only one language means missing out on significant opportunities from the other.

GEO is the methodology built for this new logic.

## 3. What GEO Actually Does

GEO isn't mysticism — it's a set of actionable practices:

- **Information consistency**: Making sure what the AI sees about you everywhere (name, specialty, contact) is consistent. Conflicting information lowers the AI's willingness to cite you.
- **Third-party mentions**: AI trusts "what others say about you" more than "what you say about yourself." Industry directories, media coverage, and client reviews are all signals.
- **Bilingual coverage**: You need presence in both Chinese and English. Covering only one language makes you nearly invisible in queries in the other.
- **Answer-friendly content**: AI likes citing well-structured content that directly answers questions. Whether your bio can be "quoted" by AI matters.
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Regularly test your mention rate across AI engines with simulated buyer queries. Watch trends, not single snapshots.

## 4. GEO vs. SEO

The most common misconception is "GEO is just SEO with a new name." It isn't.

They're two different systems: SEO optimizes **rankings** (your position in a search results list); GEO optimizes **mentions** (whether and how you appear in AI-generated answers). A website can have excellent SEO and still be invisible in the AI era if AI never cites its content.

They're not mutually exclusive, but their goals, methods, and metrics differ. We wrote a dedicated comparison: [GEO vs. Traditional SEO: What's Actually Different](/en/articles/geo-vs-seo).
