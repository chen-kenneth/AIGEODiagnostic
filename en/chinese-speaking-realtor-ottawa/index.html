Why bilingual communication matters

A real estate transaction runs on precise language — conditions, clauses, deadlines. When the paperwork is in English but your thinking is in Chinese, a bilingual realtor keeps meaning intact across both: you understand exactly what you are signing, and the other side hears exactly what you intend.

Ottawa buying process, briefly

Most purchases follow the same spine: mortgage pre-approval → budget and area → viewings → offer → conditions (financing, inspection) → lawyer closing. Timelines vary by property and market conditions.
