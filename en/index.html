FAQ

FAQ

BBuying a Home

How much down payment do I need as a first-time buyer?
Minimum 5% down on the first $500K for first-time buyers. The exact figure depends on price and mortgage pre-approval — book a free consultation.
What should I watch for during viewings?
Age, roof, heating, and basement waterproofing are the big four. We walk through each with you and arrange a professional inspection.
What costs should first-time buyers budget for beyond the down payment?
Ontario land transfer tax, inspection, legal fees, moving costs and more. First-time buyers get rebates — book a free consultation for a clear breakdown.
Can a Mandarin-speaking realtor help me buy a home in Ottawa?
Absolutely. Haiyun works in both Mandarin and English — viewings, negotiation and closing, all explained clearly in your language.
What should newcomers know before buying in Ottawa?
Build your credit history and get pre-approved early; mortgage options exist for work and study permit holders too. Start with a consultation.

SSelling Your Home

Should I renovate before selling?
Not necessarily. Staging + deep cleaning + pro photography delivers the best ROI. We advise case by case.
How is commission structured?
Discussed transparently based on the property and engagement — no hidden fees.
How is a listing price determined?
Based on recent nearby sales (CMA), property condition and market heat. We offer free evaluations — priced with data.
What should I consider when choosing a listing agent?
Look at local sales records, the marketing plan (photography, channels) and communication fit. Happy to chat first.

RLeasing

I own a rental property and need help leasing it out — do you handle that?
Yes — and it's a big part of our leasing work: rent pricing, listing photography, marketing across channels, tenant background and credit screening, lease preparation. You just collect the rent.
What documents do I need to rent?
Typically proof of income, a credit report, and references. We help you prepare everything to boost approval odds.
How can I find a rental home in Ottawa?
Tell us your budget, preferred areas and move-in date — we screen listings, join viewings and help you avoid problem rentals.
What should newcomers know about renting in Ontario?
Know your basic tenant rights, deposit rules and lease terms. We review the lease with you before you sign.

CCommercial Real Estate

Where do I start with commercial investment?
Start by defining your goal — cash flow or appreciation — then we screen by area and asset type with full due diligence.
How is commercial real estate different from residential?
Valued on rental yield rather than owner appeal, with different financing and longer due diligence. Run the full numbers before investing.
Can Haiyun help with commercial property transactions?
Investment advice and transaction assistance are available — contact us directly to confirm scope.
