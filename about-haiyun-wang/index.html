个人介绍Introduction

王海云是 Royal LePage Integrity Realty 旗下旺家地产 Upside Realty 的双语 REALTOR® 与团队负责人。他将本地市场洞察、策略性谈判与个性化服务结合，帮助客户实现地产目标：为每位客户量身定制策略、沟通清晰、诚信为本，并在交易的每个环节注重细节。作为团队负责人，他也通过领导力与指导支持团队经纪的成长。

Haiyun Wang is a bilingual REALTOR® and Team Leader of Upside Realty （旺家地产） at Royal LePage Integrity Realty. He combines local market knowledge, strategic negotiation, and personalized service to help clients achieve their real estate goals — with tailored strategies, clear communication, integrity, and attention to detail throughout each transaction. As a team leader, he also supports agent development through leadership and mentorship.

做什么What Haiyun does

Haiyun 帮助买家、卖家、房东与租客完成三类主要事项：买房、卖房与租赁，以及在做决定之前先了解房屋的市场位置（房屋估价与市场分析）。服务覆盖 Ottawa、Kanata、Stittsville、Barrhaven、Nepean、Riverside South 和 Orléans。

Haiyun helps buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants with three main things: buying a home, selling a home and leasing — plus understanding a home's market position before making a decision (home evaluation and market analysis). Service covers Ottawa, Kanata, Stittsville, Barrhaven, Nepean, Riverside South and Orléans.
